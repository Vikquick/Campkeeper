# Beta notes

*World of Warcraft: Forever* is in beta until its launch on **4 November 2026**, and Campkeeper is in **alpha** with it. Camps still change between builds, so a few things are not certain yet.

## Not confirmed yet

- **Tier 2 and tier 3 objects in Camp Benefits.** The tooltip of Camp Benefits sometimes uses a shorter name than the item (the tent shows up as *Tent*). Campkeeper matches such names by whole words; higher tiers have not been seen in a tooltip yet.
- **Range of a camp.** The aura that marks you as near a campfire disappears somewhere between 7 and 116 yards from the fire; the exact range is still being measured.
- **How long a campfire burns.** The timer for your own fire is an estimate until this is measured.
- **Blueprints for higher tiers.** How tier 2 and tier 3 objects are learned is not known yet.
- **Shared channel.** Sharing camps in the shared channel is not confirmed to reach other players in the beta client. Guild and group sharing do not depend on it.
- **English game client.** Campkeeper has been tested on a Russian client so far; English clients should work the same, as all names come from the client.

## Help make it accurate

With *Collect beta data for the developer* turned on in `/ck config`, Campkeeper notes these facts while you play. `/ck report` shows what it found; it stays on your computer. If you want to help, paste that report into an [issue](https://github.com/Vikquick/Campkeeper/issues).
