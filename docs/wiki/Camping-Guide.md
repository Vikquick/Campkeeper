# Camping guide

How camps work in *World of Warcraft: Forever*. Everything here was checked in the beta client (1.60.1); points still unknown are marked as such.

## In short

1. Someone with **Cooking** lights a campfire.
2. Players with other professions place **camp objects** next to it.
3. Everyone who **sits by the fire for 1 minute** gets **Camp Benefits** for 1 hour: the bonuses of every object in the camp.

## Campfires

A campfire is made with Cooking and placed from your bags. It works as a cooking fire, and it gives the camp its places for objects:

| Campfire | Cooking | Places |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Campfire | 1 | 3 |
| Journeyman Campfire | 140 | 5 |
| Expert Campfire | 220 | 10 |

Some guides give 90 for the Journeyman Campfire; the beta client asks for 140.

How long a fire burns is not confirmed yet.

## Camp objects

Every gathering and crafting profession, plus First Aid and Fishing, has a line of three objects:

- **Tier 1** (skill 20) usually gives a **stat bonus** to everyone at the camp: Strength, Intellect, Stamina, Spirit, mana regeneration and so on. The Leatherworking tent gives rested experience instead, the Engineering bot sells reagents.
- **Tier 2** (skill 140) and **tier 3** (skill 300) add something useful to the camp: a crafting station, a reagent maker, an ore vein, rare fish, a vendor with repairs.
- **Cooking** has the campfires instead, plus Cookie's Feast (140) and the Iron Oven (300).
- Most higher-tier objects are **placed over** the tier 1 object of their line and **keep its bonus**, so upgrading costs nothing.

The full list with numbers is on [[Camp objects|Camp-Objects]].

How to learn the higher-tier objects is not fully known yet; in the beta client the tier 2 and tier 3 blueprints did not show up in the item data.

## The bonuses

- Stand near a burning campfire and **sit down**. After **60 seconds** you get **Camp Benefits** for **1 hour**.
- The Camp Benefits tooltip lists every object of the camp with its effect.
- Crafting at the fire counts as well as sitting, according to the campfire item text.
- Each stat bonus is **mutually exclusive with a class buff**: the Lodestone with Blessing of Might, the Incense Candle with Arcane Intellect, and so on. You get one or the other, not both, so in a group with a paladin the Lodestone gives nothing.
- The **Camp Tent** gives a separate aura, **Boosted Rest**, for 1 hour: more rested experience while you rest, up to 5% of a level, once per hour.

## The one-hour cooldown

All camping items share **one cooldown of 1 hour** per character: a campfire and an object are both "camping items". So one character places **one thing per hour**.

That is why camps are a group activity: a five-person group can put up a fire and four objects at once, while a single player gets one object an hour.

## Placing objects

- An object needs a campfire **nearby** and a **free place** at that fire.
- You cannot place an object too close to another object or a creature.
- While the cooldown is running the item is not usable yet.

## How Campkeeper helps

- The **camp panel** shows the fire, the free places, what is already in the camp, and which of your items you can add right now; one click places it.
- **Timers** for sitting, Camp Benefits and the 1-hour cooldown of every character of your account.
- **Camps on the map**, shared with your guild and group.
- A **planner** that decides who in the group lights the fire and who places which object, skipping bonuses your group already has from class buffs.
